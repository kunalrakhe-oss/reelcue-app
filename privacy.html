ReelCue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

ReelCue collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDK. Your scripts and recordings never leave your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Scripts you write, paste, import, or scan are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
  • Recordings are stored in the app's private storage. If you turn on “Auto-save to Photos”, a copy is also written to your photo library.
  • Settings such as scroll speed, text size, and camera preferences are stored in the app's local preferences.

Deleting the app removes all of it. Copies you saved to Photos remain in Photos.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera — to show the preview and record video. Required to record.
  • Microphone — to record your voice with the video. Required to record.
  • Photos (add only) — to save a finished recording to your camera roll. Optional. The app can only add; it can never read or delete your photos.
  • Speech recognition — used only by the optional Captions feature, to transcribe a take you already recorded. ReelCue sets requiresOnDeviceRecognition, so transcription runs on your iPhone. If a language cannot be recognised on device, ReelCue reports an error rather than sending your audio anywhere.

What ReelCue never does

  • It does not upload your scripts, video, or audio.
  • It does not track you, in this app or across others.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it holds none.

Purchases

ReelCue offers a free 3-day trial and a one-time Lifetime purchase. Both are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. ReelCue never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details. On your device it only checks, with Apple, which of the two you own and when the trial started, so it knows when the trial ends. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

ReelCue is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
