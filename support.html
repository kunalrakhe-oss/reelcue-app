Teleprompter & video recorder for iPhone

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Email support@kunalrakhe.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Getting started

  • Tap Create New Script, type or paste your script, and save.
  • Tap Start, allow camera and microphone access, and press the red button to record. The script scrolls next to the lens.
  • Your takes appear under the script in Manage Script.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my recordings saved?

Inside ReelCue on your iPhone. Turn on Auto-save takes to Photos in Settings to also copy each take to your photo library, or share a take from Manage Script.

How do I record in landscape for YouTube?

Leave Orientation on Auto (the rotation button on the prompter screen) and turn your phone sideways; it follows the phone even with the iPhone rotation lock on. Or lock it to Landscape. The script stays centred with the controls in columns on each side.

Can I control the prompter with a remote?

Yes. Bluetooth page-turner remotes, foot pedals and keyboards work: play/pause, page back and forward, speed, and record.

How does the free trial work?

Tap "Start 3-day free trial" on the welcome screen. It is free, unlocks everything for 3 days, and ends by itself; nothing is charged and there is no subscription. After that, ReelCue Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks the app for good.

How do I restore my purchase?

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases, on the welcome/paywall screen or in Settings (gear icon) under ReelCue Lifetime. Lifetime is restored instantly, and your trial keeps its original dates. Reinstalling does not restart the trial.

Do I need an account?

No. ReelCue has no accounts and no servers. Your scripts and videos never leave your phone unless you share them.
